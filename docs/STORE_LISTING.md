## Summary (132 characters max)

## Description
PII by Punjab Insurance adds a CRM and a training centre to Gmail for partners and producers in the Punjab Insurance network. It is invitation-only: you need an invitation from your administrator.

What you can do
- Link an email conversation to a client record in one click
- Follow clients through pipelines, with tasks and reminders
- See renewals coming up in the next 30, 60 and 90 days
- Keep licences and E&O insurance current, with reminders before they expire
- Take training courses and quizzes, and download a certificate when you finish
- Log continuing education (CE) hours against your licence term and see what is still needed
- Company and branch administrators get an overview of pipeline, tasks, renewals, training and team activity
- Head office reviews partner compliance across companies (counts and files only, never client records)
- Available in English and French (Canada)

Your privacy
- Signing in uses your Google account for your name and email only. It does not give access to your inbox through the Gmail API.
- Only conversations you choose to link are stored in full.
- Your clients are visible to you and the people above you in your organization, and to nobody at your level.
- Optional AI drafting of course text and quizzes sends only the text you ask it to work on to our AI provider. It is off unless your company turns it on, and is never used to train models.
- Data is stored on secure servers in the country shown at sign-in.

## Permission justifications
- **storage:** remembers the chosen country and small display settings.
- **scripting:** lets the Gmail integration library (InboxSDK) load its page script so the PII panel can appear inside Gmail. No remote code is downloaded or run.
- **Host mail.google.com:** shows the PII panel and menu inside Gmail and reads the open conversation only when the person links it to a record.
- **Host *.punjabinsurance.co:** talks to the PII service for the person's own data (ca. and us. are the sign-in addresses).

## Data disclosures (Privacy practices tab)
- Collected: personally identifiable information (name, email), authentication information, user activity inside the product (training progress, CE log), personal communications (the sender, recipients, subject and text of an email, saved only when the person links that conversation to a record), financial and health information only if the person types it into a note or field, files the person uploads (CE proof, licence documents).
- Not sold. Not used for creditworthiness or lending. Not used for advertising. Not transferred except to our hosting and AI providers to run the service.
- Remote code: none.
