## Summary (132 characters max)
Gmail CRM for Punjab Insurance partners: track clients, renewals and licence compliance without leaving your inbox.

## Description
PII by Punjab Insurance adds a CRM to Gmail for partners and producers in the Punjab Insurance network. It is invitation-only: you need an invitation from your administrator.

What you can do
- Link an email conversation to a client record in one click
- Follow clients through pipelines, with tasks and reminders
- See renewals coming up in the next 30, 60 and 90 days
- Keep licences and E&O insurance current, with reminders before they expire
- Company and branch administrators get an overview of pipeline, tasks, renewals and team activity
- Head office reviews partner compliance across companies and countries (counts and files only, never client records)
- Available in English and French

Your privacy
- Signing in uses your Google account for your name and email only. It does not give access to your inbox.
- Only conversations you choose to link are stored in full.
- Your clients are visible to you and the people above you in your organization, and to nobody at your level.
- Data is stored on secure servers in the United States.

## Permission justifications
- **storage:** remembers the chosen country and small display settings.
- **scripting:** lets the Gmail integration library (InboxSDK) load its page script so the PII panel can appear inside Gmail. No remote code is downloaded or run.
- **Host mail.google.com / inbox.google.com:** shows the PII panel and menu inside Gmail and reads the open conversation only when the person links it to a record.
- **Host *.punjabinsurance.co (and the service addresses):** talks to the PII service for the person's own data.
