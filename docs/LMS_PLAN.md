# LMS plan, drawn from Expadio

Source: the Expadio LMS (private repo, read only). Goal: take what fits PII's multi-tenant, node-based model; skip the rest.

## Take
1. **Licence / CE status and 30/60/90 forecast, plus daily learner reminders.** Status derived from dates (valid → expiring → expired). **Done**: `packages/domain/src/forecast.ts`, `GET /v1/compliance/calendar`, "Coming up" on the Compliance page, `sweepLearnerReminders` (course due in 7 days / late; CE at risk / overdue once per term), migration 0033 `reminder_marks`, notification kinds `learning_due` and `ce_reminder` (en and fr-CA), settings `notify.learning_due`, `notify.ce_reminder`.
2. **This plan saved in docs.** Done.
3. **Versioned courses.** **Done**: publishing freezes a version (`course_versions`, immutable by trigger, migration 0034). A published course's lessons and quizzes are locked; unpublish, edit, republish releases version N+1 only if content changed. Learners who have not finished move to the new version; finished learners stay on the version they completed (`outdated` flag in `/v1/my/learning`). `GET /v1/courses/:id/versions` lists history. Not done: learners cannot keep reading version N while an author edits N+1 (unpublishing hides the course meanwhile).
4. **Assignment rules with preview.** **Done** (migration 0035): a rule covers a branch, optionally only one role, with a fixed due date or "N days after joining". `POST /v1/courses/:id/assignments/preview` shows who would be enrolled without saving; `GET .../assignments` lists rules; a 10-minute background pass re-applies every rule so people who join a branch later are enrolled too.
5. **Assessment attempt rules.** **Done** (migration 0036; rules in `packages/domain/src/assessment.ts`): pass mark, attempt cap, a cool-down between tries (`cooldownMinutes`, set on the lesson while the course is a draft), a pass ends the quiz, and each try is numbered and records the course version. Learners see attempts left or when they can retry. The cool-down is set on the lesson's quiz rules screen; a trainer or manager can give a learner extra attempts from the same screen.
6. **Learner player: lock and resume.** **Done** (migration 0037): an author can mark a course step by step (set while it is a draft); learners then cannot open or finish a lesson until the required lessons before it are done (authors are never held back). The course page has Start / Continue, which goes to the last lesson opened, or the first unfinished one. Rules in `packages/domain/src/assessment.ts`.
7. **Certificate verify page and PDF.** **Done** (migration 0038). The public page `/c/:code` already existed; it now shows the course version, and CE hours come from the version that was completed, so a later course edit cannot change an old certificate. `/c/:code/pdf` downloads an A4 PDF. Names in Gurmukhi, Devanagari and accented Latin are drawn with embedded Noto fonts.
8. **Optional enrollment requests.** **Done** (migration 0039): an author can mark a course "people must ask to join". Learners then cannot open it until approved (assigned learners are already enrolled). They send a request with an optional note; the people who lead their branch are notified and can approve or decline with a reason; the learner is notified of the answer, and a declined request can be sent again. `POST /v1/courses/:id/request`, `GET /v1/course-requests`, `POST /v1/course-requests/:id/decision`.
9. **Starter course content.** Written fresh (Expadio has none), reviewed by counsel. **Done (drafts).** Three courses (E&O basics, Privacy and client information, Ethics and fair treatment), each 3 lessons + 5-question quiz, English and fr-CA, general principles only. Authors add them from Training > Starter course; they arrive as unpublished drafts. Counsel/compliance must review before publishing.

## Skip
Competency frameworks, SCORM/LTI/xAPI, discussions and moderation, AI pipeline / outbox / content-asset system.

## Constraints
- Reminders are in-app only: there is no server-side email provider. Adding one is a separate decision.
- Server changes must stay compatible with the extension in review (1.4.4). Screens inside Gmail change only after the next batched extension update.
- Nothing here changes the extension manifest, so no new Chrome Web Store review.


## Follow-ups (done)
- **Email reminders via Resend.** Learning-due, CE and licence-expiry reminders are also emailed (en / fr-CA by the person's language), unless the person turns off "Email reminders" on their Notifications page. Needs `RESEND_API_KEY` (and optionally `MAIL_FROM`, default `PII by Punjab Insurance <notifications@punjabinsurance.co>`; the sending domain must be verified in Resend). With no key, reminders stay in-app only.
- **Unicode PDF certificates:** Gurmukhi, Devanagari and accented Latin names print with Noto fonts (no more redirect to the printable page).
- **Quiz rules in the editor** (pass mark, max attempts, wait between tries) and **managers can give a learner extra attempts**.
- **One email per person per day:** the daily run gathers a person's reminders into a single email.
- **Learner told** when a manager gives another quiz attempt.
- **Drafts hidden:** a draft course is visible only to people who can edit it, so starter courses never reach learners before they are published.

## Demo tenant

A fictional company ("Maple Demo Insurance" on pii-ca, "Liberty Demo Insurance" on pii-us) filled with sample data for demos and testing. Invite-only: only the address in `DEMO_ADMIN_EMAIL` can sign in as its admin; the other users have `@demo.invalid` addresses, so nobody can sign in as them and the mailer never sends to them. Others are invited through the normal invitation flow.

**Private demo links (no Google account):** a head-office admin creates them under Integrations > API keys > Demo access (`POST /v1/demo/invites`). Opening `/demo?invite=<token>` signs the visitor in to the demo as its admin for 12 hours; a "View as" switcher then lets them become any demo user. Links expire (1 to 30 days), cap their uses, are revocable and only their hash is stored. They work only for tenants flagged `is_demo`. Inside the demo, sending mail, inviting people, API keys, AI calls and privacy requests are refused. The Chrome extension is unchanged.

**Demo inbox:** inside the demo tenant a "Demo inbox" tab shows 11 fictional emails (English and French). The panel beside each is the extension's own sidebar code (`apps/extension/src/sidebar.ts`, imported unchanged; only its `api` import is swapped for a plain request in the web build), so linking to a record, notes, tasks, stage changes and alias-rule suggestions all run against the real API. Replies are simulated and never sent. Seeded alias rules: travel@, supervisa@, life@ at maple-demo.example.com.

- Set `SEED_DEMO=true` and `DEMO_ADMIN_EMAIL=<address>` to create it at deploy time. Add `DEMO_RESET=true` for one deploy to rebuild its CRM data, then remove it.
- Or run `node packages/db/src/cli.ts seed-demo --admin-email <address> [--reset]`.
- Data is deterministic and dated relative to today: org tree with partners, about 14 users in every role and state, 4 pipelines (one branch-only), 150 contacts, 200 deals with overdue and stale cases, notes, call logs, tasks and linked threads.
- Compliance sample: two partners (one in good standing with an E&O policy about to run out, one with an expired licence and no E&O) and a gated Life & Health stage. Client compliance sample on Life & Health: signed, waiting and draft advice records (built-in and vendor tier), consents (one withdrawn), ID checks (one failed), screening (one open hit, one reviewed), large cash (one unreported and overdue, one reported), a legal hold, and two closed files past their retention periods. Not yet seeded: LMS data and policy-issuing cases. An existing demo picks all of this up on the next reset (a reset lifts the signed-advice lock for the demo company only).
15. **Compliance: file-completeness gates.** A stage can require typed paperwork (privacy consent, brokerage agreement, needs analysis, client ID record, electronic card opt-in, beneficiary designation) before a record may enter it. **Done**: migration 0051 (`box_documents.doc_type`, `stage_doc_requirements`, `gate_overrides`), `apps/api/src/gates.ts`, `routes-gates.ts` (`GET /v1/boxes/:id/gates`, `POST /v1/boxes/:id/gate-overrides`, `GET /v1/compliance/files-at-risk`), 409 `gate_blocked` on stage moves. Only an administrator or the compliance role sets requirements or waives; waivers are permanent, named and reasoned (10+ chars), and app_runtime cannot update or delete them. Screens: editor checkboxes, typed upload and waiver form on the record Files tab, Files at risk on Compliance. Expiry alerts now 90/60/30/0 (migration 0052). "Not cleared to transact" list (`GET /v1/compliance/clearance`, domain `clearance.ts`): approval, licence in force, E&O in force and at or above the minimum; CE (previous term) is included since item 28. Advice records, client ID/AML, retention reporting and the ZIP audit package are items 16 to 19. Pipeline copies keep required docs (item 22); starter templates carry none by design (item 31).
16. **Compliance: advice records and signing tiers.** The brokerage keeps its own record of advice given (needs analysis, recommendation, beneficiary designation, replacement disclosure). **Done**: migration 0053 (`advice_records`, trigger that makes a signed record permanent), `routes-advice.ts`, domain `advice.ts`. Built-in tier: a one-time code is emailed to the client (15 minutes, 5 tries); the advisor enters it, the wording is hashed (SHA-256) and a signed copy is filed as a typed document, which also satisfies file gates. Vendor tier (beneficiary and replacement): no code route; the advisor records vendor name, reference and the completed document. Screen: Advice tab on the record. The client-facing signing page and sealed PDF came later (item 20).
17. **Compliance: client ID and AML.** Append-only records per client file: ID verification (method, documents seen by type and issuer only, verifier, date, result; ID numbers are refused), PEP and sanctions screening (a hit blocks gated stages until the compliance role or an admin clears it with a written review), and a large-cash log ($10,000 or more is flagged; the FINTRAC report reference is added once, PII does not file). **Done**: migration 0054, `routes-aml.ts`, domain `aml.ts`, ID & AML tab on the record. A verified ID record satisfies an `id_record` gate. The 5-year retention clock is in the retention report (item 19); open items now show under Files at risk (item 26). Not yet: purge, vendor ID/screening APIs.
18. **Compliance: audit package.** One-click ZIP for a client file (`GET /v1/boxes/:id/audit-package`) or a producer (`GET /v1/compliance/partners/:id/audit-package`): `summary.pdf`, `ledger.csv`, `audit-log.csv`, every supporting document as stored (client documents decrypted), `manifest.json` with a SHA-256 per file, and a README. Only an administrator or the compliance role can export; each export is audited (`audit_package_exported`). Dependency-free ZIP writer in `apps/api/src/zip.ts`. Limits: 25 MB per package, audit entries are only those the exporter can read. The summary PDF, and the advice and sealed-signature PDFs, draw Gurmukhi and Devanagari with embedded Noto fonts (a character in no font prints as ?). Buttons: record Files tab and the producer file on Compliance.
19. **Compliance: client consents, retention and overview.** Client consents (collection/use, carrier sharing, electronic messages, electronic card opt-in) with wording version (`draft-1`, pending counsel), channel and permanent withdrawal; an active consent satisfies its paperwork gate, a withdrawal re-opens it, and a withdrawn electronic-messages consent blocks emailing the client a signing code. Retention: per-company periods (defaults advice 15, AML 5, consent 3, CE 5; may lengthen, never below minimum), legal holds (placed, released, never erased), and a report of closed files that are retained, held or due for review. **Reporting only: nothing is deleted** (purge needs counsel sign-off and a Canadian storage vault). Principal Broker overview counts on the Compliance page. Migration 0055; routes `routes-client-consent.ts`, `routes-retention.ts`; domain `client-consent.ts`, `retention.ts`; screens: Consent tab, Retention and Overview on Compliance.
20. **Compliance: client signing page and sealed PDF.** An advisor sends a client a 7-day link (`POST /v1/advice/:id/signing-link`, built-in tier only; emailed unless electronic messages were withdrawn). The client opens `/sign/<token>` (English / French), gets a 6-digit code (max 5 sends, 15 minutes, 5 tries), types their name and agrees. PII seals a PDF with a signature page (signer, time, method, IP, device, content fingerprint), files it as typed paperwork and locks the record. Links are stored hashed; rate limited.
21. **Compliance: e-signature vendor options.** Catalogue of DocuSign, OneSpan Sign, Adobe Acrobat Sign, Notarius, carrier e-application, Dropbox Sign, signNow and Other (`GET /v1/compliance/signing-vendors`). Administrators and compliance turn on the ones the company uses (`PUT .../:vendor`); if none is on, all are allowed. Vendor-tier paperwork records the chosen vendor, its reference and the completed document. Recorded by hand today: live connections need each vendor's account and API credentials.
22. **Pipeline copies keep their required paperwork.** Copying a pipeline now copies each stage's required document types (previously the copy had no gates).
25. **Starter quizzes no longer give the answer away by length.** In 157 language-versions of questions across the starter courses and banks the right answer was much longer than the wrong ones. The wrong options were rewritten (English and French) to match, and a test now fails if the right answer is more than 1.25 times the longest wrong one. Draft content: counsel and a French reviewer still need to read it. Companies that already copied a starter course keep their copy; a new copy gets the balanced quiz.
23. **Archive vault (Amazon S3, write-once).** Signed advice documents (sealed PDFs and vendor-completed documents) are copied, encrypted with the company key, to an S3 bucket with Object Lock (`apps/api/src/vault.ts`). A background pass retries anything not yet copied; `vault_objects` records bucket, key, version, fingerprints and the lock-until date (append-only). Retention is the company's advice period (default 15 years). Administrators and compliance see status, can archive now, can verify a copy (fetched, decrypted, fingerprint compared), and can download a copy back out as the original file (`GET /v1/compliance/vault/:id/download`, audited, refused if the fingerprint does not match). Everything else stays on Railway. **Switch on with** `VAULT_S3_BUCKET`, `VAULT_S3_REGION` (use `ca-central-1` for the Canadian site), `VAULT_AWS_ACCESS_KEY_ID`, `VAULT_AWS_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY` and `VAULT_LOCK_MODE` (`GOVERNANCE` while testing, `COMPLIANCE` only after counsel confirms: it cannot be undone). The bucket must be created with Object Lock enabled. Nothing is ever deleted by PII.
24. **Adobe Acrobat Sign connection.** An administrator saves the company's Adobe sign-in (encrypted; never shown again) on the Compliance page. Vendor-tier paperwork (beneficiary designations, replacement disclosures) can then be sent to the client for signature through Adobe from the advice tab; the wording locks on sending. A background pass (and a Check button) brings the signed PDF home, files it as typed paperwork and locks the record. Only Adobe addresses are accepted. Tested against a stand-in for Adobe; **not yet tried against a live Adobe account**.
26. **Files at risk lists open identity, screening and cash items.** Next to the missing-paperwork list, `GET /v1/compliance/files-at-risk` now returns `aml`: a screening hit nobody has reviewed, a failed ID check that is still the latest, and a large cash receipt with no report reference (marked overdue after 15 days). Each disappears when resolved. Limited by the same visibility rules as the rest; English and French.
27. **Compliance alerts, and sweeps that include a head-office company that operates.** Administrators and compliance are told once per item about a large cash receipt 15 days on with no report reference, and a screening hit unreviewed after 2 days; and once a week while signed paperwork has waited over a day to reach the archive vault (in-app and, unless switched off, by email; two new switches under Settings > Notifications; migration 0060). The licence-expiry, learner-reminder, assignment, e-signature and vault sweeps used to skip any company flagged head office; a head office that also runs its own business (as the live one does) now gets them too.
28. Not-cleared list also flags a partner when a tracked learner (licence recorded before the last term ended) fell short of continuing education in the term that just ended. Shown as "check the rules" because counsel has not confirmed grace periods.
29. Compliance events on the record's Activity feed. The feed (`GET /v1/boxes/:id/timeline`) now also lists advice created and signed, documents filed, copies to the archive vault, consents recorded or withdrawn, ID checks, screenings, large cash receipts, paperwork waivers and legal holds, under a new Compliance filter (English and French).
